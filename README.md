A repository for our submission for the Game Development division of GEMASTIK XVIII, an IT Competition orginized by Telkom University and the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology of Indonesia. This game is made in Unity and is still in it's early prototype build.
Our team consist of:

Evan Razzan Adytaputra (Artist (Dialogue Portraits))

## Description
Play as Satria in a journey of growth and discovery. As a new student of Tunas High, you have 31 Days to make the school a better place. During this time, you'll spend your days hanging out with friends, discovering their challenges, and helping them overcome them. On the flip side, enter a mysterious world called the Dusk Zone, where environmental problems manifest themselves into mysterious creatures known as the Duskbornes. Inspired by the unique atmosphere of Yogyakarta's School Life, Satria's journey combines his school life with a battle against the unknown.
